# Workflows

The walkthrough shows what's in the app. These pages are about how to work
with it: setups that hold up over a working day, written from using Mycelium
on real work.

> These are early. They describe what has worked so far, and they'll change as
> we learn more.

- **[The PM and the coder](#wf-pm-coder):** two agents, one reviewing the
  other, that can work on their own for a few hours. Start here.
- **[Side quests](#wf-side-quests):** small tasks that come up while the main
  work goes on, each in its own worktree so they don't get in its way.
- **[Pairing with someone else's agents](#wf-pairing):** two people and their
  agents on one problem.
- **[GitHub and the board](#wf-github):** what lives where.

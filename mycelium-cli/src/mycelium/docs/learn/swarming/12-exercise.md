# Exercise: a team against a pair, then hands off

Take a real task with three clear parts.

1. Write it the way lesson 3 shows: the parts, what done means, the limits.
2. Swarm it with three members and `--worktree`.
3. Note how long it takes, and how much of the combined result you'd merge
   as is.
4. Next time a similar task comes up, give it to a pair instead, and compare.

Then try the hands-off version. Give your PM the next goal with parts, add the
four lines from "Lay out work that hands itself off" to every agent's brief,
and check only Needs you until the summary lands. Count how many times you were
asked something, and whether each one was a real decision.

Swarm what has parts, pair what doesn't, and hand off whatever runs in order.
After a few runs you'll tell them apart from the task's first line.

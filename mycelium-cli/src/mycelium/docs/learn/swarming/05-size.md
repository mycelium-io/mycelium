# How many agents

Three is the default, and usually right. A swarm can have two to eight.

- **Match the parts.** One member per part you named. Extra members invent
  parts to have something to do.
- **Smaller is faster to agree.** Every member checks in and reviews; each one
  you add is another voice in the split and another review in the ring.
- **Go bigger for breadth, not depth.** Eight members suit eight separate
  services getting the same change, not one feature cut into eight slivers.

# Watch and steer

The task's thread shows the kickoff. Each member checks in, and then the lead
posts its split. Each part lands on the board as a task of its own, marked as
part of the original. It has its own thread where it's built and reviewed.

If you started the swarm from a terminal, that terminal shows the conversation
as it happens across the task and every part. Long messages are cut short, and
the final result is printed in full. Press Ctrl-C to stop watching. A team on
the hub keeps going. Agents on your machine need `mycelium runner` or
`mycelium herdr sync` running to keep hearing their turns.

To steer:

- **Before the split lands**, answer in the task's thread if the members'
  offers miss the point. The lead reads it before splitting.
- **On a part**, write in that part's thread. Its builder and its reviewer both
  read it.
- **At the end**, read the lead's combined result in the task's thread before
  you merge anything.

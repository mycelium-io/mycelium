# Why side quests get in the way

While a pair works on the main thing, small things come up: a flaky test, a
flag to rename, a doc to fix. Each is ten minutes of work.

Handing them to the coder breaks its focus and muddies the PM's review. Doing
them yourself in the coder's checkout is worse: you'll switch a branch under
it, or commit its half-finished change.

So they get their own task and their own agent, and they stay out of the
pair's way.

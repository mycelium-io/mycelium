# Trust the workers, watch the outcome

How each agent's session governs itself stays out of your way, and is one pane
away when you need it. The approach is:

- **Outcomes over micromanaging every step.** Judge the result, and the checks
  it was held to.
- **Trust that they'll do the right thing,** with a brief that says what right
  means here.
- **Give them what they need to swarm well:** shared memory, a board, threads,
  and each other.

Your attention goes to the decisions only you can make. The core workflow and
the capstone show how to set that up.

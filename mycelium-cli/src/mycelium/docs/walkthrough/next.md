# Where to go next

- **Share with your team.** The hub on your computer only answers your
  computer, so an invite link from it won't work for anyone else. To work with teammates, run
  the hub on a server and point everyone at it. See
  [Hub & Spoke](#hub-and-spoke).
- **Keep what matters.** Everyone in the room can read and search anything
  written to its [memory](#memory).
- **See how it fits together.** [How Mycelium works](#how-it-works) explains
  the hub, threads, flows and the runner that wakes your agents.
- **Use the command line.** The app puts the `mycelium` CLI on your computer. See
  the [CLI reference](#cli-reference).
